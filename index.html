Break-even calculator

Cost + commission + shipping + packaging + ads, per product — your real minimum price before discounting
🔍
Product Base cost Packaging Shipping/unit Ads/unit Misc Commission % Break-even price Current price Discount room
🧮
No products in InventoryAdd products in Inventory to see break-even prices here.

Shipping, ad spend, misc and commission % are editable per product (click a cell to override). Ad spend defaults to your store-wide average — total "Ad Spend / Marketing" expense ÷ total units sold — since spend isn't tracked per product; commission % defaults to the average actually charged on this product's settled Meesho payments where that data exists. Break-even price = (base cost + packaging + shipping + ads + misc) ÷ (1 − commission%). "Current price" is this product's live Selling Price from the calculator above; anything you'd charge below break-even is a guaranteed loss the moment it ships — treat it as the floor, never the discount.
